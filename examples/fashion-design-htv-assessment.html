Project Brief: Custom Fashion Design Commission

StyleCraft Boutique, a trendy fashion retailer specialising in custom apparel and accessories, has commissioned you to create a unique heat transfer vinyl design that showcases contemporary fashion design techniques. Your design should be applied to an item of clothing or accessory of your choice to create an eye-catching, wearable piece that appeals to fashion-conscious customers seeking distinctive, personalised items. The piece should demonstrate sophisticated design thinking whilst being commercially viable for the boutique's diverse clientele.

This assessment follows the design thinking process to create your heat transfer vinyl fashion project. Each stage builds systematically toward your final wearable solution:

Evaluate: Six Thinking Hats Reflection (Part 1)
Instructions: Use the Six Thinking Hats method to conduct a comprehensive evaluation of your project. Consider each perspective thoroughly.
White Hat - Facts
Focus on objective information and data. What measurable facts can you report about your project? Include dimensions, materials used, time spent, costs, technical specifications, and observable outcomes. Avoid opinions or interpretations.
Red Hat - Emotions
Express your genuine feelings and emotional responses. How did you feel during different stages? What excited, frustrated, or surprised you? Include gut reactions and intuitive responses.
Evaluate: Six Thinking Hats Reflection (Part 2)
Black Hat - Critical
Identify problems, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. What didn't work as planned? What risks or limitations did you encounter? What would you warn others about? Focus on constructive criticism and realistic challenges.
Yellow Hat - Positive
Highlight successes, benefits, and positive aspects. What worked exceptionally well? What are you proud of? What value does your design bring? Focus on optimistic and constructive observations about achievements and potential.
Evaluate: Six Thinking Hats Reflection (Part 3)
Green Hat - Creative
Explore new ideas, alternatives, and creative possibilities. What innovative solutions emerged? How could you approach this differently next time? What creative modifications or extensions could you make? Think outside the box and generate fresh perspectives.
Blue Hat - Process
Reflect on your thinking and design methodology. How effective was your planning and problem-solving approach? What thinking strategies did you use? How well did you manage the design process itself? Consider your learning and metacognitive awareness.
