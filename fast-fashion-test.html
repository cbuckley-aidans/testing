Checkpoint 1 — 30 Points Total — Due: End of Week 3

A1: Fast Fashion Investigation 10 points

Learning Intention

We are learning to analyse the social, environmental and ethical impacts of fast fashion on people and the planet.

Success Criteria

  • I can identify at least three negative impacts of fast fashion
  • I can explain how fast fashion affects different stakeholders (workers, consumers, environment)
  • I can use evidence from research to support my explanations

A2: Sustainability Research 8 points

Learning Intention

We are learning to analyse how designers and businesses consider sustainability factors when creating fashion products and services.

Success Criteria

  • I can identify strategies that sustainable fashion brands use
  • I can explain how upcycling contributes to sustainability
  • I can compare sustainable approaches to fast fashion practices

Learning Intention

We are learning to use empathy research to define real-world problems and develop design criteria.

Success Criteria

  • I can gather information about user needs through interviews
  • I can create user stories that capture what a user wants and why
  • I can contribute to developing design criteria that include sustainability
