SECTION 2: Data Transmission

Total: 18 marks

2
Data Transmission 18 marks

Learning Intention

We are learning to understand how data travels across the internet, including how files are broken into packets, transmitted, and reassembled, and how encryption protects our data.

Success Criteria

  • I can create a diagram showing how data travels from my device to the cloud
  • I can explain why files are broken into packets for transmission
  • I can define encryption and explain why it is important
Q4
Packet Transmission Diagram 12 marks

Draw and annotate a diagram showing what happens when you upload a document to OneDrive.

Your diagram must include these six elements (2 marks each):
  1. Your device (laptop/iPad) with the original file
  2. The file being broken into packets
  3. Packets travelling through the network/internet
  4. Encryption being applied to packets
  5. OneDrive/Microsoft servers receiving the packets
  6. Packets being reassembled into the complete file
Tips: Use arrows to show the flow of data. Label each part clearly. You can draw this by hand and take a photo, or create it digitally.

SECTION 3: Digital Footprint & Data Permanence

Total: 10 marks

3
Digital Footprint & Data Permanence 10 marks

Learning Intention

We are learning to understand that data stored online has physical locations and that once shared, we lose control over our digital information.

Success Criteria

  • I can explain where my data is physically stored when I save to the cloud
  • I can describe what happens when data is "deleted" from cloud storage
  • I can identify ways we lose control of information once it is shared online
Q7
Physical Storage & Digital Footprint 4 marks
a) When you save a document to OneDrive, where is your data physically located? (1 mark)
b) When you "delete" a file from OneDrive, does it disappear immediately and completely? Explain what actually happens. (3 marks)
Marking criteria: Correct identification of physical servers/data centers, understanding of backups and data persistence
Q8
Loss of Control 6 marks

Imagine you create a Word document with personal information and share it with a classmate via OneDrive.

Task: Explain THREE ways that you lose control of that information once it's been shared. Consider screenshots, copies, and sharing.
Marking criteria: Three distinct valid points, demonstrates understanding of data permanence and copying, real-world awareness
