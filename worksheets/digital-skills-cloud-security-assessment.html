Project Overview

Learning Intention

We are learning to research, collaborate, and communicate complex technical concepts through video presentation.

Success Criteria

  • I can research and explain how the internet, encryption, and AI technologies work
  • I can work collaboratively in a group and maintain a progress journal

Group Size

2-3 students

Duration

3-5 minutes

Format

Video presentation (filmed explanation, screen recording with voiceover, or animated presentation)

Deliverables
  • Video file submitted via OneDrive
  • Completed research workbook
  • Group journal

Research Topics
Note: Your video must address the following five topics in a logical sequence. A planning guide is provided to you in a different document for greater guidance.
Topic 1: How the Internet Works

Research and explain:

  • How data travels in packets across the internet
  • The role of routers in directing traffic
  • Underwater internet cables connecting continents
  • How WiFi transmits data wirelessly
Topic 2: Encryption & Data Security

Research and explain:

  • What encryption is and why it's necessary
  • How encryption protects data during transmission
  • Real-world example of where encryption is used (e.g., HTTPS)
Topic 3: AI Hardware - CPUs vs GPUs

Research and explain:

  • What CPUs and GPUs do
  • Why GPUs are better for AI training than CPUs
  • Parallel processing and how it relates to AI
Research Topics (Continued)
Topic 4: What is AI & How is it Trained?

Research and explain:

  • Basic definition of artificial intelligence
  • How AI systems learn from training data
  • Examples of where AI is used in everyday life
Topic 5: AI Impacts - Ethics, Sustainability & Future

Research and discuss:

  • Environmental impact of AI data centers (energy, water, cooling)
  • Ethical considerations (bias, privacy, job displacement)
  • Predicted future impacts of AI on society
  • Possible solutions to sustainability challenges
