Solenoid Experiment 15 pts

A solenoid is a coil of wire that creates a magnetic field when electricity flows through it. This is how the clock mechanism converts electrical energy into motion.

Your Task: Build your own solenoid by wrapping copper wire around a core. Apply electricity and use compasses to observe the magnetic field you create.

B2
Define: Design Criteria 15 pts
What are design criteria? These are the rules your final product must follow. You'll use these to guide your design AND evaluate your finished clock.
Criteria Categories
Category What it means Example criteria
Function How well does it work? "Clock insert must fit securely in the centre hole"
Aesthetic How does it look? "Must match the recipient's favourite colour scheme"
Sustainability Environmental impact? "Must use sustainably sourced or recycled materials"
User Need Does it suit your user? "Must be appropriate size for their bedroom wall"
Requirements:
  • At least 1 of your 5 criteria must relate to sustainability
  • You must measure the clock insert and include this as a size constraint

SCAMPER is a creative thinking technique to help you develop and improve your ideas.

Do some quick sketches to experiment with new ideas. You do not need to commit to making one of these changes, but you are being assessed on your ability to experiment with a variety of approaches and concepts.

S - Substitute

B6
Technical Sketch 20 pts
A TECHNICAL SKETCH is a professionally done sketch which clearly outlines your final design. Another designer should theoretically be able to replicate your design by following all the information in your technical sketch.
Requirements: Your technical sketch must include:
  • Measurements (dimensions in cm or mm)
  • Labels for all key parts and features
  • Materials noted for each component
  • Centre hole size for the clock insert
  • Colour notes if applicable
