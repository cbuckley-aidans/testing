Empathy Interview Question Preparation 5 pts

In this section, you are going to prepare some purposeful questions that you can use in an interview. You are going to explore how your interviewee thinks and feels about fast fashion.

This could be anyone that you think could give you some useful information; a peer, someone in a different year level, a sibling, or a parent.

Good empathy questions are OPEN-ENDED (can't be answered with yes/no) and help you understand the person's experiences, feelings, and needs.

Problem Definition 3 pts
Step 1: Identify the Core Problem

Look back at your empathy map. What challenges did you uncover about how people relate to clothing, fashion, and sustainability?

Step 2: Write Your POV Statement
POV Statement Formula:
[User type] needs [a way to... / help with...] because [insight from your research]

Examples:

POV Statement Could lead to...
"A busy parent needs a way to give old clothes new purpose because they feel guilty throwing them away but don't have time to deal with them." Physical: Quick-to-make upcycled item
Digital: App to easily donate/swap locally
"A teenager needs help expressing their style sustainably because they want unique clothes but can't afford ethical brands." Physical: Customised clothing item
Digital: Tutorial app for DIY fashion
A4.4
User Stories 3 pts
User Story Formula:
As a [user type], I want [goal/feature] so that [benefit/reason]

Now translate your POV statement into two user stories — one for each solution you'll design.

User Story A: Physical Upcycled Item

This describes what your physical product needs to do for ONE person. Think about what type of item would solve your user's problem.

Example:

"As a busy parent, I want a durable everyday bag made from my child's outgrown clothes so that I can carry my essentials while keeping a memory of their childhood."

User Story B: Digital App

This describes what your app needs to do for MANY people. Think about how technology could help solve the same underlying problem at a larger scale.

Example:

"As a busy parent, I want an app that connects me with local people who want my child's outgrown clothes so that I can declutter quickly without the guilt of landfill."
